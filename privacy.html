het-server-backup: privacy policy

het-server-backup is a private tool used by its owner only, to store their own server backups in their own Google Drive.

What it accesses

Only the files it creates in the signed-in Google Drive (the drive.file permission). It cannot read, list or change any other file.

What it does with that access

It uploads backup archives, checks that each upload is complete, and deletes the older backups it created itself so that only the newest three remain.

What it shares

Nothing. No data from Google is sold, shared, sent to third parties or used for anything other than storing these backups. The sign-in token is kept only on the owner's own server.

How to stop it

Remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
